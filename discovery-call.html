What we cover

An online meeting to uncover your reporting problems, and whether fixing them would make financial sense.

01

Business questions

  • What are your top priorities for the next 6 to 12 months?
  • What KPIs keep you accountable?
  • What keeps coming up in leadership meetings?
  • Where do you feel you have the least visibility?
02

Decision-making

  • What decisions are difficult for you to make today?
  • Where might inaccurate information be affecting decisions?
  • What do you regularly ask your team for?
03

What good would look like

Which metrics you want to improve, and by how much.

04

Prioritisation and costing

  • If we could only solve one issue, what would it be?

We will then tell you if we think the return outweighs the cost of the work or not.
