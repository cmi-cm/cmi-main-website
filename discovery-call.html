Book a discovery call. Start with a conversation.

No pitch. No jargon. By the end of the call you'll know exactly what we'd build for your business, what it would cost, and what it would be worth.

Book your call now What to bring

What we'll cover

This isn't a sales call. It's a working session to figure out whether a project makes commercial sense for you.

Your actual business problem

Not "we need a new website", but the real issue underneath: too few enquiries, leads going to competitors, manual work eating your week.

What success looks like

We'll define it in numbers you already care about: enquiries per month, jobs won, hours saved. Not vanity metrics.

The cost of doing nothing

Every month with an underperforming site has a price. We'll put a rough figure on it so you can weigh it against the investment.

What a solution is worth

If the numbers don't stack up, we'll tell you. We'd rather turn down a project than deliver something that doesn't pay for itself.

What to bring

You don't need to prepare a thing to book. But if you have any of the below to hand, the 30 minutes will work a lot harder for you.

01

Your current website, if you have one

Just the address is enough. If you know roughly how many enquiries it brings in per month, even better. "None" and "no idea" are both useful answers.

02

Your rough numbers

Average value of a job or order, roughly how many enquiries you get a month, and how many turn into paying work. Ballpark figures are fine, this is how we work out what a solution is actually worth to you.

03

Where new business comes from today

Word of mouth, repeat customers, directories, walk-ins? Knowing what already works tells us where a website can add the most, and where it can't.

04

One clear goal for the next 12 months

More enquiries, bigger jobs, a new service line, less time on admin. One honest priority beats a wish list of ten.

05

A competitor or a site you rate

One or two examples, competitors or otherwise. What you like and dislike about them tells us more than an hour of abstract discussion.

06

Whoever says yes

If someone else signs off on spending decisions, having them on the call saves you relaying everything second-hand and saves everyone a second meeting.

Don't have all of this? Book anyway. "I don't know" is a perfectly good answer to any of these, and finding the answers together is half the point of the call.

What you get out of it

Thirty minutes, a straight conversation, and something useful to take away whether or not we ever work together:

  • A clear read on what's holding your website back
  • A rough figure for what fixing it is worth to you
  • An honest answer on whether the numbers justify the spend
  • No follow-up sales sequence, no pressure, no obligation

If a project doesn't make commercial sense for you, we'll say so on the call and you'll have lost nothing but half an hour.
