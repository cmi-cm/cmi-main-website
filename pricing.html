Why there is no price list

We could put a number on this page. It would be a guess, and we would spend the first call walking it back, which helps nobody.

Two manufacturers of the same size can be a month apart in effort. One has four systems with usable databases and a finance team who agree on what revenue means. The other has eleven years of spreadsheets, two site mergers and a product coding scheme that changed in 2019 and was never backfilled.

So we do the audit first, for free, and quote against what we actually found. You get a real number based on your real data, rather than a headline figure with a page of caveats under it.

If that sounds like a way of avoiding the question, the free phases are the answer. You can get all the way to a written, specific number without spending anything.

What moves the price

  • How many systems need connecting
  • Whether they can be read directly or only exported
  • How much history needs cleaning up
  • How many reports you actually need on day one
  • Whether access needs restricting by site or role
  • How much of it your own team wants to take on

Fixed price, not a day rate

Most data consultancies bill by the day. We think that puts the incentives in the wrong place, so here is the difference laid out plainly.

Billing by the day

  • You find out the total cost afterwards
  • Slower work earns more, which is a strange thing to reward
  • Scope creep is billable, so nobody pushes back on it
  • Learning your systems is charged to you
  • Budget approval means approving an unknown

How we do it

  • One number, agreed before work starts
  • Taking longer than expected is our problem, not yours
  • Anything outside scope is quoted before it is started
  • The free audit is where we learn your systems
  • You can take a specific figure to your board

The trade off is honest: fixed pricing means we scope carefully and say no to vague briefs. That is why the audit comes first, and why it is free.

Pricing questions

The ones people ask before they are willing to book anything.

Why are there no prices listed on this page?

Because a number without a scope is meaningless. Two manufacturers of the same size can be a month apart in effort depending on how many systems are involved and what state the data is in. Rather than publish a figure we would immediately have to caveat, we do the free audit first and then quote a fixed price against what we actually found.

Do you charge a day rate?

No. A day rate pays us for taking longer, which is the wrong incentive. Work is quoted as a fixed price against an agreed scope, so if it takes longer than expected that is our problem rather than yours.

What happens if the scope changes halfway through?

Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before it is started, so nothing appears on an invoice that you have not already agreed to. Work already scoped stays at the price we agreed even if it turns out to be harder than expected.

Are there licence costs on top?

Possibly, and we will tell you before you commit. Reporting usually runs on Power BI licences bought directly from Microsoft, and cloud hosting is billed by Microsoft rather than through us. We do not mark those up or take commission on them.

Do we have to sign an ongoing contract?

No. Support is optional and monthly, and everything is documented and handed over so your own team or another supplier can maintain it. If you never want to speak to us again after handover, the work still stands up.

What is the smallest piece of work you will take on?

A single report that somebody needs and nobody can build. Small pieces are a sensible way to find out whether we work well together before committing to anything larger.
