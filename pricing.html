What actually happens

Six steps, whichever type of project you are buying. Nothing starts until the one before it is signed off.

1

Discovery call

Thirty minutes, free. What the problem is, what fixing it would be worth, and whether it is worth doing at all.

2

Fixed written proposal

A few days later. One price, the scope in plain terms, and the reasoning behind the number. No expiry date on it.

3

Design

You see it before it gets built. Two rounds of changes are in the price, because it is cheaper to change a design than a build.

4

Build

Hand written and tested on real devices. You get progress updates without having to chase for them.

5

Launch

Domain, hosting, analytics and tracking configured and handed over, with everything documented for whoever comes next.

Support

Thirty days free after launch for anything that breaks or was not quite right. A care plan after that is optional.
