How we get to your number

The figures above are starting points. Here is exactly how they turn into a price with your name on it, and what happens at each step.

  1. A free 30 minute callWe work out what the business problem actually is, what a fix would be worth to you in enquiries or hours saved, and whether it is worth doing at all.
  2. A written quote, a few days laterOne fixed price, the scope it covers in plain terms, and the case for why the number makes commercial sense against what you told us on the call.
  3. You decide in your own timeNo follow up sequence, no chasing, no discount that expires on Friday. If it is not right, tell us and that is genuinely the end of it.
  4. Half up front, half on launchThe price does not move. If we underestimated the work, that is our problem. The only thing that changes it is a change you ask for, agreed in writing first.

You will not be quoted a price on the call itself. Pricing a project properly takes more than half an hour, and a number invented on the spot is a number invented to close you.
