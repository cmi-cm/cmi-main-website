Fixed price, not a day rate

Most data consultancies charge by the day. We charge by the project, which gives us every reason to work efficiently and means you get no surprise bills along the way.

Billing by the day

  • You find out the final cost when the work is done
  • The longer a project takes, the more you pay
  • Extra work means more charges, even when the original scope would have been enough
  • Projects commonly go over budget

How we do it

  • A fixed price, agreed before any work starts
  • We carry the risk of delays, not you
  • Anything outside scope is quoted upfront before it is started
  • No surprise bills or unexpected costs

Fixed pricing means we need to understand the work before putting a price on it. That means being careful about scope and sometimes saying no if things aren't clear enough.

Pricing questions

The ones people ask before they are willing to book anything.

Why are there no project prices listed on this page?

Because every project is different. Two manufacturers can have very different requirements depending on their systems, data quality and what they want to achieve.

Do you charge a day rate?

No. We agree on a fixed price before work starts. That means you know what you're paying upfront, and if the work takes longer than expected, that is our responsibility, not yours.

What happens if the scope changes halfway through?

If something falls outside the agreed scope, we'll explain what's involved and provide a separate quote before doing any additional work. Nothing gets added to your bill without your agreement.
