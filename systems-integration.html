What we build

We work with your existing systems. We do not replace them.

Your ERP stays your ERP. We build the layer underneath it that makes everything agree.

Data moves on its own

Information passes between your systems automatically, instead of someone copying it every Friday afternoon. Pipelines run on a schedule or in near real time using APIs, direct database connections or change data capture, and they tell you when a feed fails instead of failing quietly.

Everyone quotes the same number

One place where a customer, a job, a product and a period each mean one thing. History is kept, so this quarter and the same quarter last year can be compared without arguing over which export was used.

Every figure has an owner

You always know who is accountable for a number and where it came from. Named owners, written definitions, role based access, retention rules and a documented change process. This is the part most integration projects skip, and the reason most of them decay within a year.

Reports build themselves

The board pack arrives without anyone spending three days on it. Power BI dashboards and automated flows sit on top of the governed layer, so the numbers in the meeting and the numbers on the shop floor come from the same place.

Why data governance matters

Plenty of firms will connect two systems for you. Fewer will tell you who owns the result.

Ownership

Every dataset and every headline figure has a named person in your business who is accountable for it. Not a supplier, and not whoever happened to build the report last.

Definitions

A written catalogue of what each measure means, in plain English, agreed by the people who use it. The argument happens once, not every month end.

Access and audit

Role based permissions, so people see what their job needs. Any figure can be traced back to the system and the record it came from, which matters at audit and matters more when a customer queries an invoice.

Retention and change control

Clear rules on what is kept and for how long, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Changes to pipelines and definitions get reviewed and recorded, so nothing shifts underneath a report without anyone knowing.

Questions we get asked first

The honest answers, before you spend half an hour on a call.

Will you replace our ERP?

No. Replacing an ERP is slow, expensive and rarely the actual problem. We work with the systems you already have and build the layer that makes them agree with each other. If your ERP genuinely is the constraint, the audit will say so.

We are a small business. Is this overkill?

It depends on the pain, not the headcount. If someone is spending a day a week moving data between systems, or month end takes three days of exporting and pasting, the numbers usually stack up. If they do not, we will tell you on the call rather than sell you a project.

Do we have to move to the cloud?

Not necessarily. Plenty of this runs against on premise SQL Server. Cloud usually wins on cost and resilience once several sources are involved, but that is a decision we make in the audit with your constraints in front of us, not a default.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your tenancy, on infrastructure you own or pay for directly. We work to UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, sign a data processing agreement, and agree retention and access rules with you before the build starts.

What if we want to take it in house later?

That is a fair outcome and the build is designed for it. Standard tooling, documented pipelines, a written runbook and training for your team. There is no proprietary layer locking you to us.

How long does it take?

The audit is two to three weeks. After that we work in stages, highest pain first, so you see something live within weeks rather than at the end of a six month programme.
